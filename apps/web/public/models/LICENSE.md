# Models

All files in this folder are generated by `scripts/blender/build_models.py` (Blender 5, headless `bpy`) from primitives
modelled in that script: no third-party asset is used. They are released under the MIT licence of the repository.

Regenerate them with:

```bash
python -I scripts/blender/build_models.py --out apps/web/public/models
```

Fantasy models come from `scripts/blender/build_fantasy.py` (`make fantasy-models`).

Ocean models come from `scripts/blender/build_ocean.py` (`make ocean-models`); their colours are the real colours of the species.

Each file is one mesh with its colors baked into vertex colors (`COLOR_0`), a baked ambient occlusion, no material and no
texture.
