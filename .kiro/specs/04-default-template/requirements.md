# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Make the default template `neon-nocturne` look like `assets/reference-image-original.png` (`PROJECT.md` §2, checklist
of the `world-template` skill). The world engine (`03-world-engine-core`) already places notes, structures and arcs;
this spec tunes the camera, terrain, staff, note glyphs, city and effects of the renderer (`apps/web/src/xr`) and the
default template data, within the Quest 3 performance budget. Everything stays a template value: the renderer reads
colors from the palette roles and never hard-codes the default look.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1 — Camera and framing

**User Story:** As a viewer, I want the desktop view to frame the world like the reference image.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE desktop camera SHALL look at the plate from an isometric-like angle (elevation 25–35°, azimuth 25–40° from the
   plate's front axis) with the near corner at the bottom of the frame.
2. AT a 16:9 or 16:10 viewport the plate SHALL fill the frame width, with the near-black background visible only
   behind the far edge.
3. IN VR the camera SHALL stay driven by the headset; the diorama placement of `03-world-engine-core` SHALL not change.

### Requirement 2 — Terrain plate, cracks and edge mountains

**User Story:** As a viewer, I want a dark cracked plate with low mountains on its edges.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE plate SHALL be square, dark (`palette.terrain`), lighter toward its centre and slightly lit by the city glow,
   and SHALL look the same whatever the scene lights (unlit or baked shading).
2. WHERE `terrain.style` is `cracked_plate` THE plate SHALL show a polygonal crack network (dried-mud cells), faint at
   rest, that pulses with light on kick onsets (`03-world-engine-core` requirement 4.2).
3. WHERE `terrain.mountains` is `edges` THE plate SHALL show low rugged mountains along its edges, built from a
   deterministic height field, that avoid the zones of the template's structure kinds.
4. Bass notes mapped to `terrain_ridge` SHALL render as rugged mountain peaks in terrain colors, not as colored cones.
5. Terrain geometry SHALL be deterministic for a seed and built once per plan.

### Requirement 3 — Staff and clefs

**User Story:** As a viewer, I want a long glowing white-cyan staff crossing the plate diagonally.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE five staff lines SHALL cross the plate diagonally with a soft curve, from near the near-left edge to near the
   far-right edge, glowing in `palette.staff` with a soft halo.
2. WHERE `staff.clef` is `treble` THE staff SHALL show a Bravura G clef (SMuFL `gClef`) at both ends.

### Requirement 4 — Note glyphs

**User Story:** As a viewer, I want real music-notation glyphs that glow on the staff.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. Note glyphs SHALL use Bravura outlines (SMuFL `noteQuarterUp`, `noteEighthUp`, `note16thUp`), rendered as geometry
   (sharp at any distance in VR), one `InstancedMesh` per glyph kind.
2. Each visible glyph SHALL have a halo in `palette.staff`, without a post-processing pass.
3. THE Bravura font file and its SIL Open Font License SHALL ship in the repository; the glyph data SHALL be
   regenerated by a script from that file, never hand-edited.

### Requirement 5 — City and equalizer spikes

**User Story:** As a viewer, I want the notes to build a lit city and equalizer spikes on the left side.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. Buildings SHALL show lit windows whose size does not stretch with the building height, and a top lighter than the
   base, colored by the violet → magenta → cyan gradient (`primary` → `accent` → `secondary`).
2. Spike notes SHALL render as equalizer clusters of thin glowing needles of varied heights, additive, fading toward
   the top.
3. THE city (buildings and spikes) SHALL occupy the left part of the plate, behind the staff, with the spikes at the
   back.

### Requirement 6 — Arcs, particles and glow

**User Story:** As a viewer, I want curved light arcs and floating particles around the staff.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. Decorative light arcs SHALL be thin elliptical curves near the ground around the staff, in `accent` and
   `secondary`, brighter on beats.
2. Particles SHALL be small round glowing dots, mostly near the plate and around the staff, drifting with song time
   only (seek-exact).
3. Glow SHALL come from unlit, additive materials scaled by `fx.bloom` and the song energy; there SHALL be no
   post-processing bloom pass (it does not run in WebXR and costs a full-screen pass per eye).

### Requirement 7 — Fidelity checkpoint and budget

1. THE team SHALL compare screenshots of `/play/demo` (production build) with the reference image at several song
   times and record the remaining mismatches in `design.md`.
2. THE default world with the demo song SHALL stay under 100 draw calls with one `InstancedMesh` per shape or glyph
   kind, and under 600 triangles per instanced shape.
3. WHEN the page is opened with `?perf=1` THEN the renderer SHALL log draw calls and triangles from `renderer.info`
   once per second.
